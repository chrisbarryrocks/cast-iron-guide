About

Well Seasoned Iron is an independent site about one thing: caring for cast iron and carbon steel cookware. Our goal is to answer the questions people actually have, such as why a pan is sticky, how to get rust off, or whether soap is really off-limits, with clear steps and plain explanations.

How we write

  • We explain the reasoning behind every step so you can troubleshoot on your own.
  • We don't invent statistics or test results. Where manufacturer guidance exists, we point to it, and where practices vary, we say so.
  • We are not affiliated with any cookware brand.
  • Guides are reviewed and updated as we learn more.

Safety

Some restoration methods involve caustic chemicals or electricity. Always follow product labels and manufacturer instructions; our guides are general information.
